I am Lingxia Qiao (乔灵霞), an Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, Fudan University. Before joining Fudan University in 2025, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of California, San Diego (UCSD), under the supervision of Professor Padmini Rangamani.

I received my Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics from Peking University in 2020, under the supervision of Professor Lei Zhang. My doctoral research focused on the design principles of biological networks, particularly the mechanisms underlying adaptation, oscillation, and noise attenuation. I earned my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Beijing Normal University in 2014.

My research focuses on computational systems biology, integrating mechanistic modeling with multi-omics data to investigate the dynamics and regulation of biological networks. My current interests include mathematical modeling, network reconstruction, and regulatory mechanisms of gene regulatory, cellular signaling, and metabolic networks.
